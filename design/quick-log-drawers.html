Quick-log drawers v2

Changes from v1, per your feedback: ① the unit chips are gone — each product has one fused input: quantity on the left, unit selector attached on the right (tap it → small menu). ② type-ahead everywhere — start typing a name and a dropdown shows your previous items first, then catalog matches (for spray, whole guide mixes too). Pick from the dropdown, or keep typing to add something new. Catalog picks show as a tag you can ✕ off.
